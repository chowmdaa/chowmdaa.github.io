⚠️ Disclaimer

This service provides immigration consulting and document support only. It does not constitute legal advice or legal representation. We assist with eligibility guidance, document preparation, form completion, and application submission support.

We are not a licensed law firm, and our consultants are not licensed attorneys (asianajaja). For matters requiring legal representation — including appeals against negative decisions, asylum cases, or other complex legal proceedings — we refer clients to a licensed immigration attorney.
