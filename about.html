About Me

Hi, I'm Al Amin

An immigration and visa consultant dedicated to making the application process clear, honest, and stress-free for individuals and families.

My story

Having navigated the immigration process myself, I know how confusing forms, requirements, and official portals can be. That experience inspired me to help others take the same journey with confidence.

Today I assist clients with Finnish residence permits, family reunification, and visit visas for the UK, US, Australia, and Saudi Arabia — offering practical guidance, careful document preparation, and honest advice about each case.

Every case is personal. I take the time to understand your situation before recommending the best path forward.

How I work

1 · Listen

We start with your goals and situation — a consultation to understand your case fully.

2 · Plan

I assess eligibility and map out exactly which documents and steps your application needs.

3 · Deliver

Forms completed accurately, documents organized, and your application submitted with care.
